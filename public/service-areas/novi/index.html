Medical care delivered where Novi patients live

Novi and western Oakland County have expanded rapidly, including senior living communities that benefit from responsive medical support. The Health Watchers helps partners bring medical professionals, virtual care, care gap closure, and clinical coordination directly into the patient's home environment across Novi and Oakland County.

For patients managing chronic conditions, mobility limitations, or transportation barriers, the trip to a clinic is often the biggest obstacle to consistent care. By delivering care at home and through telemedicine, we help Novi-area partners re-engage high-needs patients and act before small problems become avoidable escalations.

Services available in Novi

Frequently asked questions

Do you provide in-home medical care in Novi, MI?

Yes. The Health Watchers partners with population health organizations, physician groups, and senior care operators to support high-needs patients in Novi and the surrounding Oakland County area through in-home medical visits, telemedicine, and senior living medical support. Availability depends on partner program design, clinician availability, licensure, patient need, and applicable regulatory requirements.

What services are available to Novi patients?

Partners serving Novi can draw on in-home medical visits, telemedicine support, population health coverage, care gap closure, senior living medical support, and program visibility and reporting.

Which areas near Novi do you serve?

In addition to Novi, The Health Watchers supports partners across Metro Detroit and Southeast Michigan, including Farmington Hills, West Bloomfield, Southfield, Troy.
